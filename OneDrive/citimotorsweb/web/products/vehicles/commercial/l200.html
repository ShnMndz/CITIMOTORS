TRITON/L200

Description:

The New Triton/L200 is the latest version of a model which celebrates its 40th birthday this year. Following its launch in Thailand, the new model will be launched in other ASEAN markets as well as Oceania, the Middle East, Europe, Africa and Latin America. Eventually it will be sold in over 150 countries around the globe. The new model brings enhancements to the durability, reliability and comfort developed over the 40 years since the vehicle was first introduced. It features powerful "Engineered Beyond Tough" design. It has a 4WD system which gives enhanced all-terrain performance, active safety and driver assistance systems and pickup truck performance and functionality.
