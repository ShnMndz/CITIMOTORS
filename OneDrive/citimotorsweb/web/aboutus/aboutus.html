CORPORATE VISION

Citimotors, Inc. is a marketing and service company engaged in the Automotive Business committed to customer satisfaction.

We are our customer's first choice in the delivery of excellent and reliable products and services.

We have a team of professional employees who are well-cared for and continue to take pride in the work that they do.

We are recognized as the Industry Leader in growth and people management.

MISSION STATEMENT

  1. Operate on a highly profitable level and maintain a stable and financially-viable business atmosphere.
  2. Maintain competence, integrity, and competitiveness to rise above industry challenges.
  3. Enhance product excellence, reliability, and customer service standards.
  4. Support the professional growth and welfare of all employees.
  5. Support worthwhile community projects and fulfill social responsibilities.

COMPANY HISTORY

In 1987, Mitsubishi Motors Philippines Corporation (MMPC) declared the need to appoint a third franchise dealer for Metro Manila to strengthen its dealership network.

On March 11, 1988, Citimotors, Inc. was established and incorporated with an authorized capital stock of Php50-million, later increased to Php130-million.

Today, Citimotors operates three strategically located outlets: Makati, Las Piñas, and Alabang — with Makati serving as the company headquarters.
